PROJECT OVERVIEW

A Focused Angular Knowledge Base

Tech Glossary Hub Angular Edition is a learning-oriented glossary that organizes essential Angular concepts into focused categories.

Instead of presenting technical terminology as a large, unstructured list, the application organizes concepts around Angular development areas such as components, templates, signals, routing, dependency injection, forms, APIs, architecture, performance, testing, and deployment.

Each glossary entry provides a concise definition, additional explanation, and practical information where applicable.
